# Background

The Geophysics Department at Colorado School of Mines has a field session every year for rising seniors to apply the skills they have learned at Mines to their own projects in the real world. Field session lasts for 4 weeks, occurring directly after geophysics students complete their junior year. The past 4 years, the department has been lucky enough to go to Steamboat Springs, Colorado to conduct geophysical surveys to study all sorts of earth science-related topics in the area. This is a great time for the students to not only use their skills in a new environment but also allows students to research areas that haven't been extensively studied. This is actually the first year that the Geophysics Department has surveyed on water, making this a very special project. 

![Where is Stagecoach Reservoir?](../figures/background/bigmap.jpeg)  
*Figure 1: Location of Stagecoach Reservoir in Routt County, Colorado. For detailed map information, see the [Stagecoach State Park Hunting Reservation map](#1) from Johnson (2020).*

Stagecoach Reservoir, located just 20 minutes south of Steamboat Springs in Routt County, Colorado, is the centerpiece of Stagecoach State Park and serves as the largest water storage facility along the Yampa River. Completed in 1989 by the Upper Yampa Water Conservancy District, the reservoir was constructed using innovative roller-compacted concrete and now has a full pool elevation of 7,204 feet, covering approximately 820 acres with a storage capacity of over 36,000 acre-feet. 

![State Park Map of Stagecoach Reservoir (reference)](../figures/background/stagecoachmap.jpeg)  
*Figure 2: Map of Stagecoach State Park, highlighting Stagecoach Reservoir.*

The reservoir provides vital water resources for agricultural irrigation, municipal and industrial use, environmental releases to support river flows, and generates hydroelectric power sufficient for about 100 homes in the Yampa Valley each year. Stagecoach Reservoir is renowned for its recreational opportunities, including trophy fishing—particularly for rainbow trout, northern pike, and walleye—boating, camping, hiking, and wildlife viewing, making it a year-round destination for outdoor enthusiasts.

![Stagecoach Reservoir](../figures/background/stagecoachpic.jpeg)  
*Figure 3: A scenic view of Stagecoach Reservoir. For more information about Stagecoach State Park, visit the official [Stagecoach State Park website](#2).*

Given the importance of Stagecoach Reservoir to the local ecosystem and economy, understanding the dynamics of water quality and movement within the reservoir is essential. The influx of nutrients from upstream sources, such as agricultural runoff and stormwater, can have significant impacts on water quality. The study of water circulation within the reservoir will help reveal how nutrients and potential contaminants are distributed, mixed, and concentrated across different regions. By investigating these patterns, researchers can better understand the reservoir's hydrodynamics, inform management strategies, and optimize efforts to protect the health of the ecosystem and its users. This research provides a foundation for future studies on water quality and helps ensure the continued safety and sustainability of Stagecoach Reservoir.
